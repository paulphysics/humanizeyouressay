✅ Step 2: How to Format Your Input Text

When using the Essay mode, properly formatting your input text is the most crucial step for achieving a high-quality result. The tool relies on specific cues to understand the structure of your document.

Headings & Subheadings: Use Markdown

To define headings, use the Markdown syntax. This is the most reliable method.

  • Title will be generated automatically.
  • `# My Main Headings` for a Level 1 Heading.
  • `## My Subheading` for a Level 2 Heading.
  • `### A Deeper Section` for a Level 3 Heading.

The tool also has fallbacks for text without Markdown: it will likely identify a line as a heading if it is in Title Case or has fewer than 7 words (and does not end in punctuation). However, using Markdown is strongly recommended for accuracy.

🤖 Step 3: A Deep Dive into Tool Modes

Each mode uses a different set of instructions for the system. Choose the one that best fits your goal.

Default Settings

Note: While you are free to select any options from the control bar, we recommend you process your text using the following settings: Standard mode, Plain Text format.

  • Standard: This mode is focused on synonym replacement and rephrasing. It is designed to make AI text sound more natural while strictly adhering to the original word count. This mode can only be used with the "Plain Text" output format.
  • Essay (Beta): This is the most powerful mode for academic writing and requires a `.docx` output format (APA, MLA, or Harvard). It performs a multi-step process:
    • Automatic Title: No need to add a title. It will be done for you, but you can edit it later if you don't like the provided one.
    • Reference Formatting: It formats the references you provide into your chosen academic style.
    • Contextual In-text Citations: It rewrites your body paragraphs to integrate relevant citations naturally.
    • Source Relevance: Make sure the sources you provide are closely related to your essay's topic. Otherwise, they will appear in the reference list but won't be used as in-text citations.
    • Beta: This is a beta feature, so users only pay for the humanization, not the addition of in-text citations or formatting of the references. Essentially, this part of the service is provided at no additional cost above humanisation charges.
  • Formatting Only (No Humanization): This mode does not rewrite your content. It is a utility for quickly formatting a paper and requires a `.docx` output format. It will perform the following actions:
    • Correct the spelling of your text to match your chosen dialect (e.g., US or UK English).
    • Generate and add a properly formatted title page.
    • Format your provided references and add them to a "Works Cited" or "References" page.
    • Add page numbers and headers according to your chosen style (APA 7, MLA 9, or Harvard).
  • AI Playground: This is an interactive chat environment where you can experiment with various AI models. It's a great space to brainstorm ideas, get writing assistance, or explore the capabilities of AI. Your conversations are saved for future reference.

📄 Step 4: Getting Your Output

Once processing is complete, you will receive your document in one of two ways:

  • Plain Text: The output appears directly on the page. Words that were changed from the original are highlighted in green. This allows you to easily review the changes.
  • .docx Format: A download link will appear. Your document will be professionally formatted and ready for review.
